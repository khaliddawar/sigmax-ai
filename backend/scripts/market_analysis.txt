John: Hi Sarah, how do you see the market right now?

Sarah: I think the S&P 500 has moved too far too quickly. It's up about 16% in just 25 days, which makes it quite overbought.

John: So are you saying we're heading for a bear market now?

Sarah: No, I wouldn't call it a bear market yet. What I expect is a consolidation or pause. Probably a 100-200 point pullback in the S&P.

John: What's your price target for that pullback?

Sarah: We could see it go as low as 5,700, maybe 5,750. But this would be a healthy correction, not the start of a major breakdown.

John: And after that pullback?

Sarah: I believe we'll see the market advance back to the highs by late May or early June. Then we might form some kind of topping pattern going into June and July, with a more significant sell cycle after that.

John: What about interest rates?

Sarah: The Fed has pretty much eliminated expectations for rate cuts this year. The market is now pricing in just one cut for 2025. I think that's actually creating an asymmetric opportunity.

John: How so?

Sarah: Well, the probability of the Fed raising rates is extremely low. But the chance of something breaking in the economy that forces them to cut rates more aggressively remains possible. So there's limited downside but significant upside potential in betting on rate cuts.
